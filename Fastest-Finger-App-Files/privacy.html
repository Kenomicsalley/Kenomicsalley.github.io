Privacy Policy for Fastest Finger

Fastest Finger is a typing and computer-skills learning game made by Kenomicsalley. This page explains what the app does with information.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to servers to send or receive personal information.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves information only on your own device (in the app's local storage): lessons completed, stars, badges, typing statistics, and, if you choose to type it for a certificate, the name you enter. This information stays on your device and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Certificates

If you create a certificate, it is made on your device using the name you typed. You decide whether to download, print or share it.

Children

The app is designed to be suitable for children. It does not ask for personal information and does not contain advertising or links to social media.
